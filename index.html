Our Story

About Rally for Green

Tejas Kumar
Founder & Program Lead
Age 16 Rising Junior FMHS JV Tennis Flower Mound, TX

I'm a 16-year-old rising junior at Flower Mound High School and a Junior Varsity tennis player for the Jaguars. When I decided to make my mark on the community, I looked for a problem that was real, local, and meaningful — and found it right on the court.

One Player.
One Big Idea.

I wanted to do more than play tennis — I wanted to use my passion for the sport to drive real environmental impact in Texas. Millions of tennis balls are thrown away every year, sitting in landfills for centuries. I saw that problem and decided to do something about it.

I launched Rally for Green by placing collection bins at schools and courts across the Dallas area, partnering with national recycling organizations, and personally coordinating donations to local animal shelters. What started as my own initiative has grown into a community-wide movement — and I'm just getting started.

I believe you don't have to wait to make a difference. You just have to serve first.

"I love tennis and I love where I live. I wanted to find a way to give back using both."

— Tejas Kumar, Founder

The Process

How It Works

01
🎾

Drop Your Balls

Find one of our collection bins placed around the community at tennis courts, clubs, and recreation centers. Drop in your used tennis balls — any brand, any condition.

02
📦

We Collect & Sort

Our team gathers the bins regularly and sorts the balls. Balls that can still bounce head to shelters. The rest go to our recycling partners.

03
♻️

Recycled & Repurposed

We ship to our partners at RecycleBalls.org and affiliate BallBack.org, where balls are repressurized or ground into rubber surfaces for courts and playgrounds.

04
🐾

Shared With Shelters

Gently used balls are donated to nearby animal shelters as toys, bringing joy to dogs waiting for their forever homes.

Why It Matters

Our Impact

🌍 Keeping Rubber Out of Landfills

Tennis balls are made from pressurized rubber and felt — materials that take centuries to break down. Every ball we collect is one less ball sitting in a landfill for generations.

🎾 New Life as Court Surfaces

Through our partnership with RecycleBalls.org, used balls are ground into crumb rubber and used to build and resurface tennis courts and playgrounds in underserved communities.

🐶 Joy for Shelter Animals

Dogs at local shelters love to play. We donate still-bouncy balls to nearby animal shelters, giving animals enrichment and comfort while they wait for adoption.

🤝 Community in Action

This program started with one young person and a big idea. Today it's growing across our community — proof that grassroots action creates real, lasting change.
